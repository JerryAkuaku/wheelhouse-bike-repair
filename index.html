Common questions

Do I need to book?

No. Walk-ins are welcome, Monday to Saturday. If you'd like to save time, send us a message first.

How long will my repair take?

Most basic repairs are done the same day, usually within an hour or two. Bigger jobs, like a gear overhaul or a full rebuild, may need the bike overnight or for a couple of days. We tell you how long when we quote the price.

Bikes kept overnight for a major repair have a storage fee of GH₵ 10 per day.

Will you tell me the price first?

Yes. We quote the price before any work starts, so there are no surprises.

What bikes do you work on?

All types: road, mountain, hybrid, BMX and children's bikes, and most major brands. If you have something unusual, send us a message first and we'll tell you if we can handle it.

How can I pay?

Cash or mobile money (MTN MoMo and Telecel Cash). You pay when you collect your bike, and the price is the one we quoted.

Do you sell parts and accessories?

Yes. We stock lights, helmets, locks, pumps and basic spare parts. If we don't have what you need, we can usually get it within a few days.
